## Orbit 4.0 (Mac)

> **New accounts need the admin's approval.** For faster approval, message me on Telegram: [@amirr_kdm](https://t.me/amirr_kdm)

### 🍎 Mac
- **Mission Control** (the old *Notes & Brainstorm*): three big doors: **Notes**, your **English exam** and **Apply**.
- **Apply** is new: keep every university and program you apply to.
  - Browse by **country → city → university** with searchable pickers, sort by deadline, fee, total cost, tuition, progress and more, in **cards or a list**.
  - Each program has its **status**, **intakes** (spring and fall, each with its own open, close, decision and course start dates), **application fee**, **funding**, **tuition and living costs** with the total for the whole program, and **requirements** (IELTS compared with your own scores, GPA, letters, GRE… add your own).
  - A **checklist of documents** for each program, with due dates and notes.
  - A **Professors** tab: research areas, whether they take students, funding, priority, contact status (including *In process*), an email log with follow-up reminders, and a button to write them an email.
  - **Deadlines** tab with everything that is due, and an **Overview** with fees, funding and progress.
- **IELTS** page: log practice tests by skill, overall worked out the IELTS way, a **target for each skill**, **categories** (Cambridge, Mocks, your own), many ways to **sort**, and an **exam report** with charts, a skills radar and the gap to your targets.
- **⌘←** goes back to the previous page, **⌘→** forward again. **⌘B** shows and hides the sidebar.
- Timer: picking a to-do no longer switches to Pomodoro, you can change between Stopwatch and Pomodoro while it runs, and a small window asks if the to-do is finished after *Finish & Save*.
- Clicking the sidebar is more reliable while the timer runs, and clicking *Mission Control* again returns to its first page.
