## Orbit 3.0.2

> **New accounts need the admin's approval.** For faster approval, message me on Telegram: [@amirr_kdm](https://t.me/amirr_kdm)

### 🪟 Windows: the 3.0 features arrive
- **Leitner Box** with topics, the 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 day sections, a daily-minimum ring, review, words and learned tabs, and **Spelling** practice by ear.
- **Countdown** with a Persian date and time picker, a pinned event, a live clock and the next event at the top of the sidebar.
- **Notchbit island**: a small always-on-top bar at the top of the screen (planet, timer, tasks, music, countdown, Next up) with its own settings page.
- **Timer**: "Are you still there?", pause in chosen programs, Pomodoro lengths on the page, the open session comes back after closing the app.
- **Weekly schedule**: an info card on every cell (notes, study time, Study now) and copy / paste of to-dos between cells and the Tasks board.
- Settings are grouped in tabs, with a day / night switch.

### 🍎 Mac
- **Still-there check** now asks once the keyboard and mouse have been untouched for the time you chose (before it needed about two minutes more), and you can set it from 1 minute.
