## Orbit 3.0.0

> **New accounts need the admin's approval.** For faster approval, message me on Telegram: [@amirr_kdm](https://t.me/amirr_kdm)

A big one for the Mac app: Orbit now lives in your notch, learns words with you, and counts down to the days that matter.

### 🪐 Notchbit: Orbit in the notch
- Your **planet** sits beside the notch. Rest the pointer on it (or swipe down with two fingers) and it opens into a small panel with the **timer**, your **tasks**, your **music** and your **countdown days**. Choose "pointer or swipe" or "two-finger swipe only".
- **Music**: cover, play / pause / next / previous. Works with Apple Music and Spotify, and through macOS Now Playing with other apps. Shows the next song's name when the track changes.
- **Volume and brightness** show in the notch (under it or beside it) with a bar that warms up with the level. Pick your own colours. Turn on *Replace the macOS panel* and only the notch shows (Orbit then asks for Accessibility permission).
- **Keyboard language**, **battery** (plugging the charger in or out, 20% and 80%), a **lock sign** when you lock the Mac, and **Next up**: a few minutes before a weekly-schedule item starts, the notch opens with a Start button.
- Optional, experimental: show the notch on the lock screen.
- Light on the battery: nothing runs in a loop; it all wakes up when macOS tells it something changed.

### 🗂 Leitner Box and Spelling
- Keep words in boxes that follow the 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 day rule. Each box is one CSV file you can open anywhere.
- **Daily review** window from anywhere (⌃⌥L), a words-to-review count beside the clock, and **Spelling** practice with a word list.
- **Topics**: group small boxes inside a general subject, e.g. "Read & Understand › Green Packaging".

### ⏳ Countdown
- Count down to exams and other days that matter, with a **Persian date picker**, emoji and colours. The nearest day shows on the sidebar.

### ⏱ Timer
- **Are you still there?** pops up if the timer runs while you are away, and stops it (keeping the time) if you don't answer.
- **Pause in chosen apps**: pick apps (Telegram…) that pause the timer while they are in front.
- Pomodoro focus and break lengths can be set right on the timer page.
- Closing the app keeps the running timer: it comes back paused, with its time.
- A to-do can have its own length: "Study" sets the Pomodoro to exactly that.

### 📅 Planning
- Weekly schedule cells show an info card when you rest on them, with a quick **Study** button.
- **Copy and paste** to-dos between schedule cells and the Tasks board.
- Settings are now grouped in tabs, and there is a day / night switch.

### 📡 Opens even when the internet is slow
- The app no longer waits on the spinner: after a few seconds it opens with what this Mac already knows.

### 🔒 Coming soon
- **Phone Sync** (the iPhone app) is shown as *Coming soon* for now.
