## Orbit 3.1.0 (Mac)

> **New accounts need the admin's approval.** For faster approval, message me on Telegram: [@amirr_kdm](https://t.me/amirr_kdm)

### 🍎 Mac
- **Mission Control** (the old *Notes & Brainstorm*): a first page with big doors. **Notes** opens the planners and notes you know. **English Exam** opens your exam (IELTS, TOEFL is coming). **Apply** is coming soon.
- **IELTS page**: log your practice tests with the band of each skill. The overall band of every test is worked out the IELTS way, and so is your overall now.
  - A **target for each skill**; the overall target comes from them.
  - **Categories** for your tests (Cambridge and Mocks to start; add your own).
  - **Exam report**: progress charts with hover details, a skills radar and the gap to your targets.
  - Delete one test, or all of them, whenever you like.
- **Timer**: picking a to-do no longer switches the timer to Pomodoro, you can switch between Stopwatch and Pomodoro while it runs, and the to-do's progress bar fills as you study (past the time it needs it turns orange).
- After **Finish & Save** on a to-do, a small window asks whether it is finished. Done to-dos are no longer offered in the timer's list.
- **⌘B** shows and hides the sidebar.
