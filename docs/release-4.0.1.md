## Orbit 4.0.1 (Mac)

> **New accounts need the admin's approval.** For faster approval, message me on Telegram: [@amirr_kdm](https://t.me/amirr_kdm)

### 🍎 Mac
- **⌘← and ⌘→** go back and forward between pages, also inside Mission Control (Apply tabs, a program and its professors, the IELTS tabs). **⌘[** and **⌘]** do the same and also work inside a text box.
- **Apply**: a **QS ranking** for each university, shown on its card and row, with two new sorts (also in the Professors tab).
- **Apply**: the country, city and university filters are now three small searchable pickers (clean with many places), also in the Professors tab. Hovering a choice lights it up.
- **Professors**: a new contact status, *In process*.
- **Layout**: tiles now fill the whole width in big windows (IELTS, Apply overview, a program), and Apply pages fit small windows (rows become two lines, filters wrap).
- Safer deleting of checklist items, deadlines and emails; a deleted program can no longer come back from a half-finished edit.
