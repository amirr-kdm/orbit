## Orbit 3.0.3

> **New accounts need the admin's approval.** For faster approval, message me on Telegram: [@amirr_kdm](https://t.me/amirr_kdm)

### 🪟 Windows
- **Timer page fits small windows**: nothing is cut off at the top or bottom any more, and the page scrolls when the window is short.
- **Island**: keeps running when you close the window (the Orbit icon near the clock opens it again or quits), five new pictures to use as its planet, and your Store planets show in the planet list.
- **Edit weekly to-dos from Tasks**: right-click one → *Edit…* to change its day, time, repeat, study time and notes, or pick **No specific time** to make it a to-do that repeats on a day without a time.
- "Are you still there?" no longer asks again right after you answer.

### 🍎 Mac
- **Keep the notch when you quit** (⌘Q), and **Open Orbit when I log in**, in Notchbit → Always on.
- Five new pictures for the notch planet (DJ Octopus, Claude on Fire, two windows, Couch Knight).
- **Edit weekly to-dos from Tasks** and **No specific time** weekly to-dos.
- The notch no longer leaves its content hanging below the black shape when you move in and out quickly.
