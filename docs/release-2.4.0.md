## Orbit 2.4.0

> **New accounts need the admin's approval.** For faster approval, message me on Telegram: [@amirr_kdm](https://t.me/amirr_kdm)

### 🎁 Gifts
- **Gift a planet or rockets to a friend** in an animated gift box (5 box styles, optional note). Open a friend's profile → **Gift**, or press **Gift** on any planet in the Store.
- The box pops up in the middle of the screen; tap it and the planet (or a fountain of rockets) comes out. Nothing reaches the receiver until they open it.
- If your friend already owns that planet, they get its price in rockets instead.
- Gifts from the admin (planets, rockets, rockets for everyone) arrive as boxes too.

### 🌌 Mythic planets
- A new **Mythic** category with animated planets: Rocket Kitty, Neon Rocket, Ring Buddy, Shuttle Orbit, Floaty Cat, Space Pup, Moon Chase, Orbit Rider and Hello Saturn.
- 14 new still planets spread over the other categories, plus **Flag Planet**, which only the admin can give.
- Ten planets that used emoji now have hand-drawn characters (bee, dino, robot, whale, snowman…).

### ✨ Galaxy
- Orbit spacing and sun size can be set **for each solar system**; moving the sliders no longer changes the planets' size.
- Much wider zoom in and out. Solar system names show on hover. Click a planet to open its own small window. "Your Store Planets" is a wrapping grid.

### 👾 Fight Club
- The admin sets the weekly prize (still shared by damage). The fighter with the most damage **wins the boss**, counted on their profile.
- A small **Boss Slayers** leaderboard.

### 🕸️ Task graph
Names, links and tasks no longer sit on top of each other, and each category's tasks stay near their own category.

### 👥 Friends
- Open any friend's profile from the Friends page.
- A timer someone forgot running no longer shows as "studying now".

### 🐛 Fixes
- Rockets shown on a friend's card now always match their real balance (after challenge and boss payouts).
- The Jade Jungle tree is no longer cut off.
