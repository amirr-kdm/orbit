## Orbit 2.4.2

> **New accounts need the admin's approval.** For faster approval, message me on Telegram: [@amirr_kdm](https://t.me/amirr_kdm)

### ⏰ Timer alarm
- When a Pomodoro focus session or break ends you now get an **alarm sound and a notification**, even if Orbit is in the background or the window is minimised.
- New alarm sounds that really sound like an alarm: **Alarm Clock** (default), **Digital Alarm** and **Siren**. The Mac system sounds are still there, and Windows has six tones.
- **Settings → Timer sound**: choose the sound, set the volume (it goes up much louder than before) and press **Test** to hear it.
- The timer keeps counting on time while Orbit is hidden.
